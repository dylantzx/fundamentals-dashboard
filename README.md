# Fundamentals dashboard: product brief
**In one line:** Shows a Secondary 1 student which basics he is shaky on and gives him something to practise, so he stops losing half a day hunting for explanations.

## Problem
Your child is a Secondary 1 student taking eight subjects, and the cost of living means you can't send him to many tuitions. When he gets stuck he watches videos on his own, which takes a lot of time and still leaves him not knowing the fundamental issue behind the question. A source that teaches the fundamentals in a way he would understand is hard to find, so with eight subjects and little time he may give up and not keep up.

## Evidence
- You saw him watching videos on how to solve questions.
- He tells you it is time consuming and still leaves him without the fundamental issue.
- Not checked yet: whether he would keep using a curated source rather than fall back on videos.

## Success
- **Metric:** minutes saved searching for resources and learning the fundamentals
- **Today:** half a day
- **Target:** not set
- **Must not get worse:** not set

## Riskiest bet
He already watches YouTube when stuck, so a curated source only helps if he actually switches to it and stays.
- **Test:** hand him one topic, don't mention the videos, and see which he reaches for.
- **Pass mark:** he does not fall back to the video and carries on with the product.
- **Result:** not run yet

## First version
1. As a Secondary 1 student, I open a dashboard of my subjects and see a rating of my fundamentals. Done when one screen shows my subjects and where I am weak.
2. As a Secondary 1 student, I pick a weak topic and start practising questions. Done when I answer the first questions without leaving to search for a video.
3. As a parent, I hand him one topic without mentioning videos and note what he reaches for. Done when I have written down whether he stayed.

## Walkthrough
1. He logs into the app with an account.
2. He sees a list of subjects to choose from.
3. He chooses the subject he wants to practise.
4. He sees a list of questions and starts practising.

If it goes wrong: he sees more generic questions or guidance until the app has learned enough to curate the material for him.

## Not building
- Content for all eight subjects, one topic is enough for the test.
- Video lessons of our own.
- Reports over time beyond the rating.

## Open questions
- What number of minutes saved counts as success?
- What must not get worse?
- How is a fundamentals rating worked out, and does it tell him what to practise?
